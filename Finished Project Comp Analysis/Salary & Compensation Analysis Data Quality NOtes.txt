Salary & Compensation Analysis: Data Quality Notes

 Data Quality Issues Identified

During the analysis, several potential data-quality issues were identified:

- Missing values:** Some records have missing experience-year information.
- Potential salary outliers:** Base salaries of $450,000 and $1,000 were flagged for investigation.
- Unknown education:** Some records have an education level listed as “Unknown.”
- Compensation mismatches:** Four records had total compensation values that did not equal base salary plus bonus.

Handling Decisions

- Missing experience values were excluded from analyses requiring experience data.
- Potential salary outliers were retained pending further investigation.
- Unknown education values were kept as a separate category.
- Compensation mismatches were flagged rather than automatically corrected.

Limitations

Unusual salary values and unresolved compensation mismatches may affect summary statistics and visualizations. Findings should be interpreted with these limitations in mind.

Tools Used

- Excel: Data cleaning, PivotTables, and charts
- SQL (SQLite): Filtering, aggregation, grouping, sorting, and joins
- Power BI: Compensation dashboard and visual analysis
